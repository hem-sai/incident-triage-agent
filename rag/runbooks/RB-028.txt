# RB-028: Outlook shows Disconnected or Trying to connect (email)
Categories: email

## Steps
1. Confirm the Outlook status bar says "Disconnected" or "Trying to connect" and whether Outlook on the web (webmail) opens.
2. If webmail also fails, check the internet connection and the VPN. If webmail works, the problem is in the Outlook app.
3. In Outlook, check that "Work Offline" is not switched on, then close Outlook fully and open it again.
4. Create a new Outlook profile (Control Panel, Mail, Show Profiles, Add) and set the account up again.
5. If it still fails, escalate to the email team with the account name and the exact status message.
