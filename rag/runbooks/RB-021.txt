# RB-021: laptop will not power on (hardware)
Categories: hardware

## Steps
1. Confirm the laptop shows no lights and no screen when the power button is pressed, and note whether the charger light is on.
2. Plug in the original charger, try another wall socket, and leave it charging for 15 minutes.
3. Unplug the charger, hold the power button for 30 seconds to drain leftover power, plug the charger back in and press power.
4. Remove any docking station or USB devices, then try again.
5. If it still does not start, escalate to hardware support for a replacement or repair, and note the asset tag.
