# RB-007: shared mailbox full (email)
Categories: email

## Steps
1. Confirm it is the shared mailbox that is full: the error names the shared mailbox address, and the user's own mailbox still receives mail normally.
2. In the email admin portal, find the shared mailbox and compare its size with its quota.
3. Ask the mailbox owner to archive or delete old items, starting with Deleted Items, Sent Items and large attachments.
4. If the data must stay, ask the email admin to raise the quota or enable an archive mailbox for the shared mailbox.
5. Verify new mail arrives in the shared mailbox and the error is gone.
