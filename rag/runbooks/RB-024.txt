# RB-024: USB keyboard or mouse not working (hardware)
Categories: hardware

## Steps
1. Confirm the keyboard or mouse does not respond and whether the laptop's own keyboard and trackpad still work.
2. Unplug the device and plug it into a different USB port. For wireless ones, replace the battery and check the receiver is plugged in.
3. Try the device on another computer to tell a faulty device from a faulty port.
4. In Device Manager, uninstall the device, unplug it, restart the computer and plug it back in.
5. If the device fails on another computer too, replace it through the hardware request process.
