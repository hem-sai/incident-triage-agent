# RB-029: expected email not received (email)
Categories: email

## Steps
1. Confirm the sender's address, the date and time sent and the subject, and whether other mail arrives normally.
2. Search all folders, including Junk and Deleted Items, for the subject and sender.
3. Check the user's inbox rules and blocked sender list for a rule that moves or deletes this mail.
4. Ask the email admin to run a message trace for the sender and time, and to check the quarantine.
5. Release the message from quarantine if found. If the sender got a bounce, handle it as a bounce.
