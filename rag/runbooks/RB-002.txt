# RB-002: Account locked after password attempts (access)
Categories: access

## Steps
1. Confirm the account is locked after too many wrong password attempts: the login error says "account locked" or "too many attempts".
2. Verify the user's identity as per policy, then unlock the account (help desk or directory admin console).
3. Reset the password and ask the user to sign in with the new one.
4. Verify access to the system. If the account locks again, look for old saved passwords on the user's phone or other devices.
