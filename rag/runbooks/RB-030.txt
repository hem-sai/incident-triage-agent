# RB-030: calendar invites not syncing to the phone (email)
Categories: email

## Steps
1. Confirm the invite shows on the laptop calendar or webmail but not on the phone, and note the phone type and mail app.
2. Check the phone has internet and that calendar sync is switched on for the work account in the mail app settings.
3. Open the mail app and force a refresh. Check the account's sync period is not set too short.
4. Remove the work account from the phone and add it again.
5. If it still does not sync, escalate to the email team with the phone model, app version and the missing invite.
