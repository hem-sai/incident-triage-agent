# RB-026: no audio or microphone in Teams meetings (software)
Categories: software

## Steps
1. Confirm whether the user cannot hear others, cannot be heard, or both, and whether other apps play sound.
2. In the Teams meeting, open device settings and select the right speaker and microphone. Check the microphone is not muted, in Teams or on the headset.
3. In Windows settings, check the app is allowed to use the microphone and the correct input device is the default.
4. Sign out of Teams, quit it completely, and join the meeting again. Clear the Teams cache if the problem returns.
5. If the headset works on another computer but not here, escalate to desktop support to check the audio driver.
