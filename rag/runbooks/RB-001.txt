# RB-001: VPN keeps disconnecting (network)
Categories: network

## Steps
1. Confirm the VPN keeps disconnecting: note how often it drops and whether other apps (browser, Teams) lose connection at the same time.
2. Check the internet connection itself: switch from Wi-Fi to a wired connection, or to another network, and reconnect the VPN.
3. Restart the VPN client and reconnect. If it still drops, update or reinstall the client, then restart the computer.
4. If it still keeps disconnecting, escalate to the network team with the drop times and the VPN client log.
