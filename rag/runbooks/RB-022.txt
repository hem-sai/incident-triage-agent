# RB-022: external monitor not detected (hardware)
Categories: hardware

## Steps
1. Confirm the laptop works but the external monitor shows "No signal" or is not listed in display settings.
2. Check the monitor is on and set to the right input, and reseat the cable at both ends. Try a different cable or port.
3. Press Windows key + P and choose Extend or Duplicate. In display settings, click Detect.
4. Update the graphics driver and restart the laptop with the monitor connected.
5. If a dock is used, try connecting directly. If it still fails, escalate to hardware support.
