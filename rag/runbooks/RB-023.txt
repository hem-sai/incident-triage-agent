# RB-023: laptop battery not charging or drains very fast (hardware)
Categories: hardware

## Steps
1. Confirm the symptom: the battery icon says "plugged in, not charging", or the charge drops within an hour.
2. Check the charger is the original one with the right wattage, and the cable and socket are not loose.
3. Check battery health in the power report (`powercfg /batteryreport`) and look at the design capacity versus the full charge capacity.
4. Check which apps use the most power in Settings, Battery, and close or remove them.
5. If the battery health is low or it will not charge with a good charger, escalate to hardware support for a battery replacement.
